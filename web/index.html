YOUR KEY. YOUR CHOICE.

Fix the test, not just the flags.

Gemini can repair questions, answers and scoring against the original papers, then approve and export tests that pass every check.

Your Gemini connection

Loading settings…

Session keys disappear when the app closes. Saved Windows keys are encrypted on this device.

What Gemini fixes

Misread text, question numbering, answer-choice formatting, incomplete extraction and scoring that can be resolved from the published rubric.

Two checks before approval

One request repairs the complete test. A second checks the proposed result against every supplied PDF page. Local checks then validate answers, scores, coverage and metadata.

What is sent to Google

The selected test, key, supporting PDFs, extracted draft and flags. This uses your API quota and may cost money. It never runs just because you uploaded a file or saved a key.

Missing PDFs, unsupported answers and unresolved scoring stay blocked. AI approval is labeled as AI review; it is not a guarantee of factual accuracy or 2027 rules compatibility.

Repair a batch

Choose tests that need review. Up to 20 tests per run, processed one at a time. Each test normally uses two paid generation requests. No automatic paid retries.

Your Firecrawl connection

Use a Firecrawl API key, separate from your Gemini key. Get one from Firecrawl.

What happens

Search up to 20 selected tests at once. The app checks the public archive first, then searches for missing filenames. Exact checksum matches attach automatically and queue local extraction.

Other readable PDFs are saved for preview and confirmation. Use the sign-in controls below for Scioly account downloads. Unsupported external file hosts and uncertain matches stay unresolved. No Gemini repair starts automatically.

Firecrawl receives the test titles, years, filenames and public source URLs. Searches use your Firecrawl credits: at most one archive request plus 16 search/page requests per test. Signed-in browsing uses additional Firecrawl credits. No paid requests repeat automatically after closing the app.

KNOW WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Your files. Your review. Your pace.

A local preparation tool for archived Science Olympiad practice tests.

What this app does

It extracts numbered questions, multiple-choice options, written answer text and point values from PDFs. Scanned pages use OCR running on your computer. Each paper gets a saved draft and a review checklist.

It prepares practice content for Akoizo. It is not the application students use to take or submit practice tests.

What it needs

The test paper, ideally its answer key, and any separate diagram or image sheets. PDF files must be unlocked. You also need enough free disk space for the original files and drafts.

The packaged desktop version includes its runtime. The source version needs Python 3.11+ and the dependencies in the setup guide. Internet is needed for initial setup and optional Gemini repair. Local extraction works offline. Uploaded PDFs and saved reviews return automatically when you reopen the app.

Three things we flag

Missing answers No key, absent answers or question/key numbering that doesn't match.

Unclear formatting Scans, columns, repeated numbers, diagrams, ambiguous choices or multipart questions.

Conflicting scores Missing points, disagreements between paper and key, or a total that doesn't add up.

What needs a person

OCR can misread a symbol. A published key can be wrong. A physical specimen may not be included. Automated checks do not prove that a draft is scientifically correct or complete.

Compare the entire draft with the source, correct the answers and scores, and record how each flag was resolved. Reviewed exclusions should have zero points and an explanation.

Keep it running

Once started, the converter watches the inbox and works through its queue, one paper at a time. Minimizing the desktop window keeps it running. Closing the desktop app stops it safely. Use Pause to finish the current paper and wait, or Stop app to exit.

Restart it after a reboot with Start Converter. Interrupted jobs resume, completed drafts are skipped, and manual review edits are preserved.

After review

Approval is tied to the exact saved draft. Editing it requires approval again. Export creates a bundle with the test data, original PDFs and review evidence.

The bundle still needs validation and import into Akoizo. This app never modifies or publishes the website by itself. Written student grading stays in Akoizo's separate Auto Grade flow.
